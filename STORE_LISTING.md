## Full Description (4000 chars max)

**Spam Shield** is a clean, elegant call blocker that lets you take control of who can reach you. Create flexible blocking rules using exact numbers, prefixes, suffixes, patterns, or even regex - and never be bothered by spam callers again.

**Powerful Blocking Rules**
- Block exact phone numbers
- Block by prefix - stop all calls from a specific area code
- Block by suffix or pattern - catch variations of spam numbers
- Advanced regex support for power users
- Quickly add rules from preset templates

**Dashboard at a Glance**
- See how many calls you've blocked today, this week, and all time
- View your recent blocked call history
- Know instantly if call screening is active

**Simple Setup**
- One-tap to set as your default call screening app
- Works automatically in the background with zero battery drain
- No account required - everything stays on your device

**Privacy First**
- 100% offline - no data leaves your phone
- No analytics, no tracking, no ads
- All data stored locally in SQLite

**Designed with Care**
- Clean retro pastel aesthetic
- Smooth animations throughout
- Playfair Display + Inter typography

**Good to Know**
- Android may skip call screening for contacts saved in your phone book
- Works best on Android 10+ with CallScreeningService support
- Some manufacturer skins (MIUI, HyperOS) may require manual setup via Default Apps settings

---

**Spam Shield** is free and will never show you an ad. Built with React Native.
